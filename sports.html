How can you do sports ? 💪

For students:

Organizing your sports routine is like building a well-structured program; it works best when the components are logical and consistent. To determine when to do sports, look for a "reboot" window—typically
in the late afternoon after school—when your brain needs a break from technical tasks or
studying. Regarding what sports to do, it is helpful to mix strategic activities like fencing or martial arts, which require intense focus and calculation, with high-energy team sports like football or basketball to balance your solo interests. You can get started by joining a local sports club, signing up for school teams, or even practicing basic drills at home using digital tutorials to master the mechanics of the game. By treating your physical activity as a vital part of your daily "system," you ensure that your body stays as sharp and capable as your mind.

For adults:

Transitioning from a student schedule to a professional one requires a shift in how you maintain your physical "hardware." To decide when to engage in sports, aim for the bookends of the day: either a pre-work session to jumpstart your energy levels or a post-office slot to effectively "clear the cache" of the day’s mental load. When selecting what sports to pursue, consider a mix of high-efficiency activities like HIIT or swimming for time-starved days, and socially-driven sports like padel, tennis, or cycling clubs, which provide a community outside of the workspace. You can implement this routine by utilizing specialized tracking apps to monitor progress, joining corporate leagues, or keeping a dedicated gym
bag in your car to minimize any friction in your daily logistics.
By integrating sports as a non-negotiable line item in your personal management system,
you ensure sustained peak performance in both your career and your health.

How can you sleep better ? 🛏️

To ensure you are performing at your best, it is vital to understand that the quality of your rest depends heavily on both timing and duration. For most people, aiming for 7 to 8 hours of sleep is the ideal window to allow the brain to fully cycle through its restorative stages. When you sleep, ideally starting between 9:00 PM and 11:00 PM, your body synchronizes with its natural internal clock, making it much easier to enter the deep sleep necessary for physical repair and the REM sleep needed for mental clarity. By combining a consistent bedtime with those 8 hours of rest, you provide your body enough time to flush out toxins and organize the information you learned during the day, ensuring you wake up feeling sharp and fully recharged.

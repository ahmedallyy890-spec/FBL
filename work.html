How can you organize your work / study ? 📝

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management system designed to maximize focus and prevent mental fatigue by breaking work into manageable intervals. The standard approach involves working with total concentration for 25 minutes, followed by a 5-minute break. These intervals, known as "Pomodoros," encourage a sense of urgency that discourages multitasking and procrastination. After completing four cycles, you take a longer restorative break of 15 to 30 minutes. By structuring your day this way, you train your brain to stay on task while ensuring regular intervals of rest to recharge your cognitive energy.

Tip: The beauty of this method lies in its flexibility. If 25 minutes feels too short for a complex task, many people find success with a 50/10 ratio (50 minutes of work followed by a 10-minute break). The goal is consistency and rhythm, not rigid adherence to a specific clock.

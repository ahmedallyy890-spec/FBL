How can you organize your food ? 🍝

To organize your eating habits effectively, focus on high-quality proteins like grilled chicken or eggs and complex carbohydrates such as rice or potatoes, which provide steady energy without the need for heavy sauces. Since you prefer specific textures, incorporating simple, dry dishes or items like pizza allows you to meet your nutritional needs while enjoying your meals. It is often most effective to eat frequent, smaller portions throughout the day—starting with a light meal in the morning and a final balanced plate about three hours before sleep—to maintain focus and strength. By keeping these preferred foods prepped and visible in your kitchen, you can ensure you’re getting the right fuel even when you don't feel particularly hungry.
